## Welcome to Skyfication!

**Skyfication** is a modern **Minecraft 1.21.1 Skyblock** pack focused on tech, automation, and smooth progression. Starting from a single platform in the void, your goal is to build complex factories, generate infinite resources, and automate your way to an advanced sky empire.

<br>

### ⚙️ What’s inside the pack?

<br>

* ⚙️ **Kinetic Automation with Create:** Built around **Create 0.6** and its best addons (*Create Sifter*, *New Age*, *Enchantment Industry*, *Copycats*, *Design 'n' Decor*). Automate early-game resource processing using kinetic energy and mechanical contraptions.
  
* ⚡ **Heavy Industry & Power:** Transition into high-tech power grids with **Mekanism**, **Ender IO**, **Powah!**, and **Immersive Engineering**. Generate massive amounts of RF, process ores efficiently, and build automated machine setups.
 
* 🪨 **Skyblock Resource Loop:** Early resource generation is powered by **Ex Deorum** and **Ex Machinis**, giving you a structured way to turn gravel, sand, and dust into raw materials and ores.
  
* 🎒 **Digital Storage & Logistics:** Manage millions of items using **Refined Storage** (with full *EMI*, *JEI*, and *Mekanism* integration) and **Sophisticated Backpacks** for upgraded early-game inventory management.
  
* 🛠️ **Pipes & Transport:** Route items, fluids, and energy seamlessly across your island using **Pipez** and **Refined Storage** cables.
  
* 🚀 **QoL & Smooth Performance:** Built on **NeoForge** with **Sodium**, **Iris**, **Lithium**, and **ModernFix** for high FPS. Includes **Forgiving Void** (no losing items when falling), **Squat Grow**, and **EMI / JEI / Jade** for quick recipe lookups.
