## Welcome to Skyfication!

**Skyfication** is a tech-focused Skyblock modpack built around the mechanics of **Create** and **Mekanism**. Forget magic blocks and digital storage networks—here, every resource loop must be engineered using gears, belts, and massive train networks directly over the void.

### ⚙️ Core Features:
* **Kinetically Automated Skyblock:** Generate and sift resources from nothing using **Ex Nihilo** seamlessly automated by **Create: Sifting** rotational force.

* **From Steam to Nuclear Power:** Convert mechanical rotation into electrical energy via **Create: Crafts & Additions** to fuel high-tier **Mekanism** and **Powah!** factories in the late game.


*Built for engineers who love to see their machines in motion. Start with a single tree, end with a fully automated, synchronized industrial empire.*
